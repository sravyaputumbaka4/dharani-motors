Who we are

From one dealership to a growing commercial-vehicle network.

Dharani Motors began its Piaggio journey in Bhimavaram in February 2025 and expanded with a field-first approach: understand the market, build customer trust, strengthen finance relationships, and execute consistently.

FEB 2025

Bhimavaram — The beginning

Started operations with a clear focus on customer service, market coverage and retail execution.

2025

West Godavari expansion

Expanded the network across Eluru, Tadepalligudem, Jangareddygudem, Tanuku, Chintalapudi, Digamarru, Kovvuru and surrounding markets.

2026

Mahabubnagar — Next chapter

Extended the business journey into Mahabubnagar, taking the same operating discipline and customer-first approach to a new market.
